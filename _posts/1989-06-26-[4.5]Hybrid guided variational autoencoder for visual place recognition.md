# [4.5] Hybrid guided variational autoencoder for visual place recognition

- Authors: Ni Wang, Zihan You, Emre Neftci, Thorben Schoepe
- [arXiv Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09248v1)
- [PDF Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.09248v1.pdf)

## Subfields
 Localization / Visual Place Recognition (VPR)
## Reason for Interest

1. **Direction**: The paper addresses Visual Place Recognition (VPR) using event cameras and SNNs, which is a relevant technology stack for the perception/localization modules of autonomous systems.
2. **Relevance**: The research explicitly targets 'indoor environments' and 'mobile robots' (Turtlebot). It acknowledges that existing work focuses on outdoor/automotive VPR, but specifically chooses to fill the gap for indoor datasets. Consequently, it lacks direct validation for car-end scenarios (high speed, outdoor lighting, city-scale).
3. **Experiments**: Validation is limited to a small-scale custom dataset (1 room, 16 cells) and a few unseen rooms. There is no comparison on standard autonomous driving benchmarks (e.g., Oxford RobotCar, Nordland).
4. **Score**: While the method (Guided Hybrid VAE) is innovative, the strict lack of outdoor/automotive validation limits its score to the non-automotive cap (≤ 5.0).
