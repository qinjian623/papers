# [9.2] LatentVLA: Efficient Vision-Language Models for Autonomous Driving via Latent Action Prediction

- Authors: Chengen Xie, Bin Sun, Tianyu Li, Junjie Wu, Zhihui Hao, XianPeng Lang, Hongyang Li
- [arXiv Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05611v1)
- [PDF Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.05611v1.pdf)

## Subfields
 End-to-End Autonomous Driving / Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models
## Reason for Interest

The paper introduces a novel 'Latent Action Prediction' framework that effectively addresses key bottlenecks in applying VLMs to autonomous driving: annotation cost, linguistic bias, and inference latency. By using self-supervised latent actions and knowledge distillation, it achieves SOTA performance on NAVSIM while ensuring real-time efficiency (4.71 FPS vs 1.26 FPS for the teacher model). The experiments are comprehensive, covering both closed-loop planning (NAVSIM) and open-loop generalization (nuScenes), with clear ablation studies validating the design choices.
