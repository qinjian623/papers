# [6.2] Mixed Precision PointPillars for Efficient 3D Object Detection with TensorRT

- Authors: Ninnart Fuengfusin, Keisuke Yoneda, Naoki Suganuma
- [arXiv Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12638v1)
- [PDF Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.12638v1.pdf)

## Subfields
 3D Perception / Model Quantization & Edge Deployment
## Reason for Interest

The paper addresses a critical industry problem: deploying LIDAR models (PointPillars) on edge hardware (Jetson Orin) using TensorRT. 

Strengths:
1. Practical Value: The focus on TensorRT and mixed precision (INT8/FP16) is highly relevant for real-world autonomous driving stacks where latency is critical.
2. Engineering Validity: Extensive hardware benchmarking (Jetson Orin, RTX 4070Ti) confirms the efficiency gains.

Weaknesses:
1. Outdated Benchmarks: For a paper dated 2026, evaluating *only* on the KITTI dataset (2012) is a significant drawback. Modern research requires validation on NuScenes or Waymo.
2. Architecture Limitation: The study focuses exclusively on PointPillars (2019). While industry-standard, it lags behind modern Transformer/BEV-based research architectures.
3. Theoretical Depth: The finding that 'fewer calibration frames improve PTQ' is useful but relies on outlier avoidance rather than robust distribution handling.

Overall, it is a solid engineering paper for optimizing legacy stacks but lacks the novelty and experimental breadth (modern datasets/models) expected of high-impact research.
