# [8.4] Mirage: One-Step Video Diffusion for Photorealistic and Coherent Asset Editing in Driving Scenes

- Authors: Shuyun Wang, Haiyang Sun, Bing Wang, Hangjun Ye, Xin Yu
- [arXiv Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24227v1)
- [PDF Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.24227v1.pdf)

## Subfields
 Generative Data Augmentation / Video Object Editing
## Reason for Interest

The paper presents a highly relevant innovation for autonomous driving data generation: a one-step video diffusion model (Mirage) that effectively inserts 3D assets into driving videos while maintaining temporal coherence and spatial fidelity. Key contributions include a 'temporally agnostic latent injection' strategy to resolve VAE compression artifacts and a 'two-stage alignment' pipeline to handle 3DGS distribution mismatches. The experiments show clear quantitative improvements (vFID, warping error) over the recent state-of-the-art (R3D2). However, the score is capped because the evaluation focuses solely on visual/reconstruction quality; there is no experimental validation showing that this synthetic data improves downstream perception model performance (e.g., object detection mAP), which is the ultimate measure of value for AD data augmentation.
