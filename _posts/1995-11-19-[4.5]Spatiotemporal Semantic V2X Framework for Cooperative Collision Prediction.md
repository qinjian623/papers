# [4.5] Spatiotemporal Semantic V2X Framework for Cooperative Collision Prediction

- Authors: Murat Arda Onsu, Poonam Lohan, Burak Kantarci, Aisha Syed, Matthew Andrews, Sean Kennedy
- [arXiv Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.17216v1)
- [PDF Link](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.17216v1.pdf)

## Subfields
 V2X Cooperative Perception / Collision Prediction
## Reason for Interest

The paper proposes a Semantic V2X framework using the V-JEPA foundation model to reduce bandwidth while predicting collisions. 

Pros: 
1. Novel application of V-JEPA for semantic communication in V2X scenarios.
2. Addresses the critical issue of bandwidth and latency in cooperative perception.

Cons (Major): 
1. **Experimental Incompleteness**: The validation relies entirely on a very small-scale custom synthetic dataset (QLabs, 500 clips). There is no evaluation on standard real-world V2X datasets (e.g., DAIR-V2X, V2X-Seq), which significantly limits the credibility of the collision prediction results.
2. **Lack of Baselines**: The paper only performs ablation studies (comparing different internal post-processing methods like heatmaps/masks) and compares bandwidth against 'raw video'. It fails to benchmark against existing state-of-the-art trajectory prediction or accident anticipation algorithms.
3. **Sim-to-Real Gap**: Purely synthetic training without domain adaptation experiments makes the practical deployment value questionable.
